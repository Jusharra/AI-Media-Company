# YouTube Video Script Template

## Purpose
A structured interview/documentary-style video script for The SIGNAL Sessions YouTube channel. Designed for 8–15 minute founder story videos optimized for YouTube SEO, watch time, and LinkedIn repurposing.

---

## Metadata Block
```
TEMPLATE: youtube-script
DOC_TYPE: youtube-script
SUBJECT: {founder_name}
COMPANY: {company_name}
INDUSTRY: {industry}
EPISODE_TITLE: {title}
RUNTIME_TARGET: 8–12 minutes (approx 1,200–1,800 words spoken)
THUMBNAIL_HOOK: {thumbnail_text}
SEO_KEYWORDS: {keyword_1}, {keyword_2}, {keyword_3}
UPLOAD_TITLE: {seo_optimized_title} | The SIGNAL Sessions
```

---

## Pre-Production Notes

**Video style**: Interview-style with B-roll prompts. Can be recorded via Riverside.fm or in person.
**Host**: SIGNAL editorial team (or direct-to-camera monologue format)
**Graphics**: Name lower-third at intro, company logo, key stats as on-screen text
**Music**: Subtle instrumental under intro/outro only

---

## Script Structure

---

### [00:00–00:30] COLD OPEN — The Hook

**Direction**: Open on the most compelling claim, outcome, or moment from the interview. This is NOT an intro — it's a preview of the best 30 seconds.

**Format**:
> [FOUNDER NAME, intercut or direct quote]:
> "[Most compelling line from the interview — specific, surprising, or provocative]"
>
> [HOST V.O. or TEXT CARD]: "This is how [FOUNDER] [achieved outcome]."

**Examples of strong cold opens**:
- A counterintuitive claim: "I turned down a $10M contract because I knew it would kill the company."
- A specific result: "In 14 months, we cut construction delays by 38% on 200 sites."
- A challenge: "Everyone said the hospital would never buy software. They were wrong."

---

### [00:30–01:30] INTRO SEQUENCE

**[HOST or TITLE CARD]**:
> Welcome to The SIGNAL Sessions. I'm [HOST NAME]. SIGNAL covers the founders, operators, and innovators building the next generation of healthcare, energy, and construction companies — before the mainstream catches on.
>
> Today: [FOUNDER NAME], [TITLE] of [COMPANY]. [One-sentence setup of why this story matters now].

**[LOWER THIRD GRAPHIC]**: `[FOUNDER NAME] | [TITLE] | [COMPANY]`

---

### [01:30–03:30] ORIGIN STORY — Before the Company

**Interview questions (choose 2–3)**:
1. "Take me back to before [COMPANY] existed. What were you doing, and what did you see that others weren't seeing?"
2. "What's your background — and why does it actually matter for what you're building?"
3. "What was the moment you knew you had to build this?"
4. "What did you know from [previous role/experience] that gave you conviction when everyone else was skeptical?"

**B-roll prompts**: [Industry footage / founder in context / early photos if available]

**On-screen text**: Key career milestone or credential

---

### [03:30–06:00] THE PROBLEM & THE INSIGHT

**Interview questions (choose 2–3)**:
1. "Explain the problem you're solving like I've never seen your industry before."
2. "What does [industry] keep getting wrong about [problem area]?"
3. "What was the insight — the thing you saw — that made you believe this could be solved?"
4. "Why hasn't this been solved before? What changed that made it possible now?"

**On-screen text**: Key statistic or market context
**B-roll prompts**: [Problem illustration / industry context footage]

---

### [06:00–08:30] THE BUILD — How They're Doing It

**Interview questions (choose 2–3)**:
1. "Walk me through how [COMPANY] actually works. What are you building?"
2. "What's the hardest technical or operational challenge you've had to solve?"
3. "Tell me about a specific customer or project that proved the model works."
4. "What have you gotten wrong, and how did it change your approach?"

**On-screen text**: Key traction metric, customer outcome, or milestone
**B-roll prompts**: [Product demo / customer footage / team footage if available]

---

### [08:30–10:30] THE STAKES — Why It Matters

**Interview questions (choose 2)**:
1. "If [COMPANY] succeeds at scale — what changes for [patients / workers / the industry]?"
2. "What does the [industry] look like in 10 years if this works? What if it doesn't?"
3. "Who benefits most from the status quo staying broken?"
4. "What would you tell a [clinician / engineer / contractor] watching this right now?"

---

### [10:30–11:30] THE FUTURE — What's Next

**Interview questions (choose 2)**:
1. "Where is [COMPANY] in 12–18 months? What are you focused on right now?"
2. "What do you need — talent, partners, customers — to get there?"
3. "What would you tell someone thinking about building in [industry]?"

---

### [11:30–12:00] OUTRO

**[HOST or FOUNDER CLOSING]**:
> [1-2 sentence summary of why this story matters].
>
> Find [FOUNDER NAME] on LinkedIn at [URL]. [COMPANY] is at [WEBSITE].
>
> If you found this valuable, subscribe to The SIGNAL Sessions — new episodes every [CADENCE]. And if you're an operator building something significant in healthcare, energy, or construction, apply to be featured at signal.media/get-featured.

**[END CARD]**: Subscribe button, next episode preview, SIGNAL logo

## Thumbnail Brief

**Layout**: Split — founder headshot left, bold text right
**Text**: 2–4 words. Counterintuitive or specific. Examples:
- "$0 to $4M ARR"
- "They said impossible"
- "38% fewer delays"
- "Fired at 30. Built this."
**Background**: Dark (navy or charcoal) with amber/gold accent text
